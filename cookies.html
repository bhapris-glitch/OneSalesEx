Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy describes cookies and similar technologies used by the zavoka AI website and Shopify storefront widget. For other information handling, see our Privacy Policy.

Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small browser file. The zavoka AI website uses browser local storage for essential site operation and, only after you allow optional preferences, to remember your selected display currency and cache currency-conversion rates. The site also stores your consent choice for up to 180 days so it does not repeatedly ask. Local storage is similar to cookies but is not itself a cookie.

The Shopify storefront widget is a separate experience running on a merchant’s store. It stores pseudonymous visitor and conversation identifiers in local storage for chat continuity and may write those identifiers to Shopify cart attributes for order attribution. The zavoka.com banner does not control the merchant storefront; merchants should disclose the widget’s storage and configure any consent controls required for their storefront and jurisdiction. Clearing browser storage can reset chat continuity or affect attribution.

The website does not use advertising cookies or a third-party analytics pixel. It requests font resources from Google Fonts and may request exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API; those providers may receive technical browser/network information when requested. The zavoka backend API is hosted on Google Cloud Run, which may process request data and operational logs needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the Service. The public website host is configured separately from the backend. See our Subprocessor and Third-Party Services Disclosure for provider and location details. Shopify and other third parties may use their own technologies when their services are used; their practices are governed by their own notices.

Why we use these technologies

  • Essential site functionality: maintain required merchant account/session state and remember whether you selected essential-only or optional preferences.
  • Optional preferences: remember your display-currency selection and cache currency rates, but only after you consent.
  • Storefront widget: maintain chat continuity and, where enabled, support merchant-facing attribution on the merchant’s Shopify store.
  • Third-party delivery: retrieve fonts and exchange rates and provide Shopify features requested by merchants.

We do not use the website’s local storage for advertising profiles, and the website does not run advertising or analytics cookies. If our technology or purposes change, we will update this policy and provide controls where required by law.

Your choices

On zavoka.com, choose Essential only to refuse optional currency-preference storage, or Accept all to allow it. Use the Cookie settings control in the site footer to change your choice; consent is saved for up to 180 days. Essential storage is always active because it is needed for core site functions. You can also clear or block browser storage, though that may reset your preference or sign you out. The website’s banner does not make consent choices for a merchant’s Shopify storefront; merchant-store widget storage and attribution are described above.
