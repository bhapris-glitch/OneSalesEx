ABOUT ZAVOKA

Transform every store visit into a revenue opportunity.

Customers expect timely answers, relevant recommendations, and a shopping experience that understands what they need. Zavoka helps Shopify merchants meet those expectations with an AI-powered Sales Executive that supports shoppers from product discovery through checkout.

Our mission

Our mission is to make helpful, personalized sales assistance accessible to every Shopify merchant. Zavoka is designed to engage store visitors, respond to their questions, guide product discovery, and help them make confident purchasing decisions—around the clock.

Our vision

We believe the next generation of ecommerce will be shaped not only by better storefronts or more traffic, but by how thoughtfully each store can engage its visitors. Our vision is to give every Shopify business an always-on digital Sales Executive that can understand a shopper’s needs, recommend relevant products, and make the path from discovery to checkout more responsive.

By connecting conversations with products, store information, and the shopping journey, Zavoka helps create a more useful experience for customers and a clearer view of customer activity for merchants.

Built for growth

Bringing visitors to a store is only the beginning. Zavoka helps merchants make the most of meaningful moments throughout the shopping journey—from a first product question to a returning shopper or an unfinished cart.

  • Engage: Start helpful conversations when shoppers need assistance.
  • Understand: Use the conversation and shopping context to respond more relevantly.
  • Recommend: Help shoppers discover products that fit what they are looking for.
  • Support decisions: Answer questions and guide customers toward checkout with confidence.
  • Learn and improve: Give merchants tools and insights to better understand store activity.

Intelligence that works with your business

Zavoka is designed to work alongside merchants—not replace them. Your products, brand, policies, and customer experience remain at the center. Our tools help extend your ability to assist shoppers, while keeping merchants in control of their store settings and customer-facing information.

Commerce features can also support key parts of the customer journey, including checkout, order tracking, cart follow-up, and store analytics. Availability depends on the store’s configuration and enabled integrations.

Our approach

Powerful technology should feel practical, not complicated. We focus on building ecommerce tools that fit real merchant workflows and are straightforward to configure as a business grows.

  • Intelligent: Designed to consider context, not just keywords.
  • Practical: Built around everyday ecommerce needs and workflows.
  • Scalable: Made to support businesses as their products and customer activity grow.
  • Merchant-focused: Created to work with the tools and decisions merchants already manage.
  • Customer-centric: Focused on helping shoppers find answers and products with less friction.

The future of ecommerce is conversational

Online shopping does not have to leave every customer to search, compare, and decide alone. A more conversational experience can listen, understand, and guide—helping shoppers move forward at their own pace. Zavoka is building toward a future where businesses of every size can offer thoughtful sales assistance without the cost and complexity of a traditional sales team.

More helpful conversations. Better shopping experiences.

Our goal is to help merchants unlock more value from the visitors they already have. Every question can be a chance to help. Every product discovery can bring a shopper closer to the right choice. And every useful interaction can lay the foundation for a lasting customer relationship.

Building the future of AI-powered commerce

Zavoka is building tools for the next generation of Shopify businesses—helping merchants provide responsive sales assistance, support customers throughout their shopping journey, and operate with greater confidence.

The store of the future won’t simply wait for customers to buy. It will understand them, assist them, and help them move forward.
